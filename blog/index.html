Home About WayOfPy

Hi, I'm Fauzan.

I build software, study systems, and enjoy understanding how things work from first principles.

By profession, I'm a software engineer. By curiosity, I'm a lifelong student of programming languages, runtime systems, and software architecture. I'm particularly fascinated by Python not just how to use it, but how it works internally.

I'm building WayofPy, an initiative dedicated to helping engineers understand Python deeply through first principles, internals, and hands-on engineering.

Outside of Python, my interests span photography, filmmaking, design, and the craft of building thoughtful products. I believe the best engineers are not only builders but also communicators and lifelong learners.

This website is my personal home on the internet - a place to share what I'm building, what I'm learning, and the ideas that shape my work.

If anything here resonates with you, I'd be glad to connect.
